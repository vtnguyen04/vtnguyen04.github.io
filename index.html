UniQuery: Cross-Domain Pedestrian Attribute Recognition

Thanh Nguyen Vo*, Nguyen Hoai Thuong Bui*, Trinh Tra Giang Nguyen*, Ha Duc Bui†

Under Review at IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV) Workshops, 2027

Co-adapts self-supervised DINOv3 (ViT-H+/16) and vision-language CLIP (ViT-L/14) with an attribute-specific joint query head for cross-domain pedestrian attribute recognition:

  • Dual Foundation Encoders: Unites spatial patch representations from DINOv3 (ViT-H+/16) with semantic vision-language tokens from CLIP (ViT-L/14).
  • Dynamic Token Routing: Pre-LN cross-attention query head over 228 multimodal tokens routes color attributes to CLIP and structural/clothing attributes to DINOv3.
  • Cross-Domain Generalization: Outperforms static single-encoder and late-fusion ensembles across 40 pedestrian attributes on unseen surveillance camera domains.
  • Dual foundation encoder uniting DINOv3 (ViT-H+/16) & CLIP (ViT-L/14)
  • Dynamic cross-attention token routing across 40 surveillance attributes
  • Paper under review at IEEE/CVF WACV 2027 Workshop

LENS: Attribute-Based Person Retrieval on Unseen Cameras

Thanh Nguyen Vo*, Nguyen Hoai Thuong Bui*, Trinh Tra Giang Nguyen*, Ha Duc Bui†

Under Review at IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV) Workshops, 2027

Formulates attribute-based person retrieval on unseen surveillance cameras through likelihood scoring and zero-cost transductive neighbor smoothing:

  • Exact-Query Log-Likelihood Scoring: Replaces heuristic L1 distance ranking with calibrated probabilistic log-likelihood evaluation over multi-teacher attribute predictions.
  • Group Cross-Entropy Training: Models mutually exclusive semantic attribute groups to suppress conflicting predictions under cross-domain camera shifts.
  • Zero-Cost Transductive kNN Smoothing: Reuses forward-pass DINOv3 patch tokens to smooth gallery logits across nearest neighbors without extra backbone inference.
  • Exact-query log-likelihood scoring over traditional L1 metric formulation
  • Zero-cost test-time transductive kNN logit smoothing via DINOv3 patch tokens
  • Paper under review at IEEE/CVF WACV 2027 Workshop

JEPA Guided Diffusion: Predictive Vision-Language Conditioning for Generative Traffic Forecasting

Trinh Tra Giang Nguyen*, Thanh Nguyen Vo*, Nguyen Hoai Thuong Bui*, Ha Duc Bui†

Proceedings of the European Conference on Computer Vision Workshops (ECCVW), 2026

Decouples traffic-world dynamics understanding from pixel-level video synthesis by bridging frozen V-JEPA 2.1 with frozen Cosmos-Predict2.5:

  • Latent World Representation: Extracts spatiotemporal traffic dynamics from observed video clips using frozen self-supervised V-JEPA 2.1 encoders.
  • Lightweight Predictor Bridge: Trains a QFormer and Llama-3.2-1B alignment module with Hungarian set-matching loss to predict future cross-attention conditioning tokens.
  • Generative Video Continuation: Guides frozen Cosmos-Predict2.5 diffusion synthesis without running heavy 7B VLM reasoning at inference (Rank 3 Worldwide, ECCV 2026 Workshop).
  • Decoupled forecasting framework combining frozen V-JEPA & Cosmos-Predict2.5
  • Lightweight predictor bridge trained with Hungarian set-matching loss
  • Paper accepted at ECCV 2026 Workshop

An Integrated Perception-to-Planning Framework for Autonomous Driving using YOLOv11 and Frenet

Thanh Nguyen Vo, Trinh Tra Giang Nguyen, Lu Anh Khoi Le, Ha Duc Bui†

2026 8th International Conference on Green Technology and Sustainable Development (GTSD), IEEE, 2026

Real-time multi-task perception-to-planning architecture deployed on NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano for autonomous urban driving:

  • Shared YOLOv11 Backbone: Jointly executes multi-scale traffic object detection and drivable corridor feature extraction in a single forward pass (35+ FPS).
  • Attention Heatmap Prior: Trains a full-resolution spatial attention branch with Dice and Focal loss to anchor trajectory prediction along sharp road curvature.
  • Command-Gated Frenet/Bézier Planning: Modulates waypoint regression with high-level navigation commands to synthesize smooth, kinematically feasible trajectories.
  • Published in IEEE GTSD 2026 Proceedings
  • Command-Gated trajectory planning with Dice loss attention prior
  • Deployed on NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano with 35+ FPS multi-task execution
